# Ergonomic review inputs: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The business, the people, the task, the measurements and the dates below are all invented. The business is an invented 30-person home-goods fulfilment company. Its warehouse supervisor is preparing for an external ergonomics assessor, after the packing team raised aching shoulders at a team meeting. First come the inputs the supervisor supplied, in full, then the record the capability returned for the assessor.

## The inputs

### 1. Referral brief

```
Task: Packing a boxed table lamp into a shipping carton at pack station 3
Location: Warehouse, pack station 3
People who do it: Packers (4 across two shifts)
Why referred: Packing team raised aching shoulders at the team meeting
  on Thursday 5 November 2026; assessor visit booked
Observed: Tuesday 10 November 2026, 07:30 to 08:30
Observed by: Warehouse supervisor
Assessor: External ergonomics assessor, visit on Tuesday 17 November 2026
Prepared for: the assessor
Shift and break pattern: 07:00 to 15:30; breaks of 15 minutes at 10:00 and
  30 minutes at 13:00
Working time on the station per shift: 6.0 hours (from the rota; excludes
  breaks and other duties)
```

### 2. The steps of the task

```
1. Take a flat carton from the stack on the shelf beside the bench, fold it
   and tape the base
2. Lift the lamp box from the trolley to the bench
3. Wrap the lamp and place it in the carton with paper fill, close the lid
   and tape it
4. Lift the packed carton onto the roller conveyor beside the bench
```

### 3. Observation notes (typed by the supervisor)

```
Observed two packers, A and B, from 07:30 to 08:30 on Tuesday 10 November.
Packer A finished 20 cartons in the hour, packer B finished 22. I timed
packer A's steps with a stopwatch on five cartons; the figures below are
typical of those five.

1. Flat cartons are on a shelf to the right of the bench. The packer
   reaches up to take one, stands at the bench, folds it and tapes the
   base. About 25 seconds.
2. Lamp boxes arrive on a trolley parked by the bench. The packer lifts
   one box from the trolley, which sits low, and puts it on the bench,
   about two steps away. The boxes are about 3 kg. About 3 seconds.
3. Standing at the bench, the packer wraps the lamp in paper with both
   hands, reaching right to the paper dispenser, places it in the carton,
   closes the lid and tapes it. This is the longest step. About 120
   seconds.
4. The packer lifts the packed carton off the bench and puts it on the
   conveyor on their left, about one pace away, turning at the waist with
   feet kept in place. About 4 seconds.

Breaks are taken at 10:00 and 13:00 as on the rota.
```

```

- Both: shoulders ache by late afternoon.
- One: it is worst when reaching up for cartons on the shelf.
- One: twisting round to the conveyor feels awkward.
- Tomas says his left wrist has been sore since September and he has seen
  his doctor about it.
- Both say the 10:00 break is often skipped when orders are running late.
- Suggestions: one would like the cartons on a lower shelf; one would like
  the conveyor turned to face the bench.
```

# Emergency drill brief: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The company, the site, the roles, the arrangements, the findings, the dates and the times below are all invented, and the arrangements are not a model for any real site. The site is an invented packaging workshop with a first-floor office, run by an invented business called Company A. First come the inputs the site manager supplied, in full, then the brief the capability returned.

## The output

**EMERGENCY DRILL BRIEF: fire evacuation, Unit 4, Company A (workshop and first-floor office)**

Date: Wednesday 18 November 2026, 10:30 to 11:00. Prepared: Tuesday 10 November 2026, from the drill details, the arrangements list (7), the roles list (4), the previous findings (3).
Approver: Site manager. Drill lead: Operations coordinator.
Scenario: approved by the Site manager on Monday 9 November 2026 (as supplied).
Announced in advance to: Site manager, drill lead, the two observers. Not announced to: everyone else.
Expected at roll call: 34 (workshop 26, office 7, visitors 1).
Counts: arrangements 7; roles 4; previous findings 3; observation points 7. Flags: 4.

**The question this drill answers**

Does everyone reach the assembly point, and is everyone counted, within our targets when exit D2 cannot be used? Source: supplied.

**The scenario**

A fire is reported in the glue store at the back of the workshop. Exit D2 is unusable. The office must leave by the internal stair. A visitor is signed in.
Pretended: the fire in the glue store, and exit D2 being unusable.
Real: the alarm sounds, people leave and assemble; nothing is lit and nothing is moved.
Stop the drill if: a real emergency happens, anyone is hurt, or the drill lead calls it off.

**Run sheet**

1. 10:15. Put a "blocked" sign across D2; the door stays closed. By: Drill lead. Arrangement: A03.
2. 10:30. Sound the alarm from the call point by the front desk. By: Drill lead. Arrangement: A01.
3. From the alarm. The workshop leaves by D1. By: Workshop fire warden (R01). Arrangement: A02.
4. From the alarm. The office leaves by the internal stair, out through D1. By: Office fire warden (R02). Arrangement: A04.
5. 10:35. Everyone is at the assembly point (target: 5 minutes after the alarm). By: everyone on site. Arrangement: A05.
6. 10:38. Roll call complete (target: 8 minutes after the alarm). Each fire warden counts their own area; the visitor log is brought to the assembly point. By: Workshop fire warden (R01), Office fire warden (R02) and Visitor log holder (R03). Arrangement: A06.
7. After the roll call. The all clear is given in person; nobody re-enters before it. By: Site manager (R04). Arrangement: A07.

**Gaps in the arrangements list**

- Help to leave for people who cannot use the stairs or need assistance: no arrangement in the list. FLAG, arrangement not supplied. See decision 1.

**Roles for the drill**

- R01 Workshop fire warden. Duty: clears the workshop and counts people at the assembly point. Holder: Shift supervisor. Deputy: Machine operator lead. On the day: neither reported away.
- R02 Office fire warden. Duty: clears the office and counts people at the assembly point. Holder: Accounts clerk. Deputy: Receptionist. On the day: holder away (Monday 16 to Friday 20 November 2026, as supplied); deputy not reported away. FLAG, holder away. See decision 2.
- R03 Visitor log. Duty: takes the visitor log to the assembly point. Holder: Receptionist. Deputy: Operations coordinator. On the day: neither reported away.
- R04 Site manager. Duty: gives the all clear. Holder: Site manager. Deputy: none. On the day: not reported away.

**Observers**

- Maintenance technician: workshop and assembly point. Holds no duty in the roles list.
- Receptionist: office. Holds a duty: R03 holder, and deputy for R02, which covers the Office fire warden on the day. FLAG, observer holds duties. See decision 3.

**What to observe**

- O01 Alarm (A01). Watch for: whether the bell is heard at the observer's position; anyone who says they did not hear it. Record: heard yes or no at the position, and the number of people who report not hearing it. Observers: Maintenance technician (workshop), Receptionist (office). Previous finding: F03.
- O02 Exit D1 (A02). Watch for: the workshop leaving by D1. Record: the time the first and the last person pass D1, and whether a queue forms. Observer: Maintenance technician.
- O03 Exit D2 (A03). Watch for: before the start, the floor zone in front of D2 (from F02); during the drill, whether anyone goes to D2. Record: the zone clear or not clear at 10:15, and the number of people who go to D2. Observer: Maintenance technician. Previous finding: F02.
- O04 Office route (A04). Watch for: the office leaving by the internal stair and out through D1. Record: the time the last person leaves the office. Observer: Receptionist.
- O05 Assembly point (A05). Watch for: people arriving at the visitor car park by the gate. Record: the time the last person arrives, against the 10:35 target. Observer: Maintenance technician.
- O06 Roll call (A06). Watch for: each fire warden counting their own area; the visitor log arriving. Record: whether the visitor log is at the assembly point when the roll call starts, the time the roll call completes against 10:38, and the total counted against 34 expected. Observer: Maintenance technician. Previous finding: F01.
- O07 All clear (A07). Watch for: the all clear given in person; anyone going back in before it. Record: the time of the all clear and the number of people who go back in before it. Observer: Maintenance technician (area taken from the run sheet).

**Findings from earlier drills**

- F01 (2 March 2026). Roll call for the office took 9 minutes because the visitor log was left at reception. Action: visitor log kept in a grab folder by the front desk. Status: done. To look at on this drill: O06.
- F02 (2 March 2026). Exit D2 was blocked by pallets. Action: floor zone marked in front of D2 and checked weekly. Status: open. To look at on this drill: O03.
- F03 (2 March 2026). Two people on the first floor did not hear the alarm. Action: check the bell volume in the meeting room. Status: not stated. To look at on this drill: O01. FLAG, status not stated. See decision 4.

**Debrief questions** (Wednesday 18 November 2026, 14:00)

1. O01. Who did not hear the alarm, and where were they?
2. O02. Did the workshop use D1 only? Was there a queue at D1?
3. O03. Was the floor zone in front of D2 clear before the start? How many people went to D2, and what did they do next?
4. O04. Did everyone in the office leave by the internal stair, and who was last out?
5. O05. How long did the last person take to reach the assembly point, against the 5-minute target (10:35)?
6. O06. Was the visitor log at the assembly point when the roll call started? Did the count match the 34 expected, and was the roll call complete by 10:38?
7. O07. Did anyone re-enter before the all clear, and how many?
8. What happened that nobody planned for?
9. What did people tell you they found confusing?
10. Which observations need an action, who owns each, and by when? The Site manager decides.

**Decisions for the Site manager: 4**

1. Arrangements, help to leave not supplied. The arrangements list has no arrangement for people who cannot use the stairs or need help to leave, so the brief cannot set an observation point for it. Is there one that should be added to the list, and should it be observed?
2. R02, holder away. The Accounts clerk, the Office fire warden, is away on the day (Monday 16 to Friday 20 November 2026). The deputy is the Receptionist, who is also the office observer. Who counts the office depends on it. Does the Receptionist cover R02 in this drill, or is someone else named?
3. Observers, observer holds duties. The Receptionist is the observer for the office, holds R03 and is the deputy for R02. Who observes the office, if the Receptionist is carrying the visitor log and counting the office?
4. F03, status not stated. The finding has an action and no status, and what O01 looks at depends on it. Has the bell volume in the meeting room been checked? If not, O01 will look at it as an open action.

**Not covered**

This brief does not direct a live emergency, does not demonstrate rescue capability, is not an emergency plan and does not say what any law or regulator requires. It does not check how much one observer carries, whether two observation points fall at the same moment in different places, or whether an absence is inside the expected numbers; the Site manager looks at those when approving. It records what to watch and ask; the Site manager decides what the drill showed.

# Workplace risk assessment: an invented example

*Illustrative example with invented data.* The business, the people, the site, the log entries and the dates below are all invented. The business is an invented 40-person garden-supplies wholesaler. Its site manager is assessing one task: unloading a delivery of bagged compost at the warehouse and putting it away. First come the inputs the site manager supplied, in full, then the draft the workflow template returned for the competent person to review.

## The inputs

### 1. Scope brief

```
Task: Unloading a curtain-sided delivery of bagged compost at unloading
  bay 2, and putting it away
Location: Warehouse yard and goods-in, unloading bay 2
People who do it: Warehouse supervisor (1), Forklift drivers (2),
  Warehouse operatives (6)
Others who may be affected: Visiting delivery drivers
How often: Five deliveries a week, between 7:00 and 10:00
Assessment date: Monday 9 November 2026
Run by: Site manager
Competent person: External health and safety adviser
Review interval: 12 months
Rating scale: none of our own; use the template's default
```

### 2. The steps of the work

```
1. Reverse the trailer into bay 2, guided by a marshal
2. Open the curtain and check the load
3. Unload the pallets by forklift to the marshalling area in goods-in
4. Break down mixed pallets by hand and stack the bags on the bottom
   shelf of the racking
```

### 3. Walk-round notes (typed by the site manager)

```
Walk-round, Thursday 5 November 2026, with the warehouse supervisor and
two operatives.

1. Yard: trailers reverse into bay 2. The warehouse supervisor, or an
   operative when the supervisor is busy, marshals each one in. Most
   trailers have a reversing alarm, not all. Everyone wears hi-vis in
   the yard. There is no marked walkway. Staff walk across the yard to
   the smoking shelter by the gate, straight through the area where the
   trailers reverse.
2. Curtain: the visiting driver opens the curtain. We tell drivers to
   look before they open it. Pallets of compost sometimes arrive
   leaning against the curtain.
3. Forklift: the forklift takes pallets from the side of the trailer to
   the marshalling area inside the goods-in door. That area is also the
   way staff walk to the racking. No floor markings. Our two forklift
   drivers are both trained; the training records are in the office.
4. Putting away: mixed pallets are broken down by hand and the bags
   stacked on the bottom shelf. Bags are 25 kg each (from the delivery
   note), about 40 bags by hand per delivery. Manual handling is covered
   at induction. Priya hurt her back doing this in June and was off for
   a week. I'd rate this one likely (4) and serious (3).
5. Agreed so far:
   - Barriered walkway along the building wall, and the smoking shelter
     moved beside the office: Site manager, by 15 December 2026. Budget
     agreed.
   - Floor markings and a barrier between the walkway and the forklift
     drop area in goods-in: Warehouse supervisor, by 27 November 2026.
   - A lift table for breaking down mixed pallets: Site manager. Needs a
     quote first, so no date yet.
   - Ask the compost supplier to deliver pallets stretch-wrapped and
     strapped: not sure who should own this, maybe purchasing.
```

No other records were supplied. No High deadline was given, so the default of 30 days applies.
